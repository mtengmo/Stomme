# Additional exercise views

This change adds five front projections and two oblique projections. They are
alternate drawings of the existing movements; exercise instructions, doses,
equipment choices, and progression rules remain unchanged.

| Reference | Exercise | View | Projection note | Existing source recorded for the movement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E06 | Stationärt utfall (split squat) | Sned vinkel | Separates the forward support foot from the receding rear foot; the rear contact plane is marked. | [ACE Exercise Library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/) |
| E18 | Farmarbärning (farmer carry) | Framifrån | Shows both hands carrying one implement each while the legs alternate. | [ACE farmer's carry](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/359/farmer-s-carry/) |
| E24 | Utfall bakåt | Framifrån | Keeps the selected forward foot in place while the other foot steps to the receding contact plane. | [Catalyst Athletics reverse lunge](https://www.catalystathletics.com/exercise/735/Reverse-Lunge/) and [ACE walking lunge reference (PDF)](https://contentcdn.eacefitness.com/cp/pdfs/FitnessMatters/Jan10.pdf) |
| E25 | Utfall framåt | Framifrån | Keeps the rear foot on the receding plane while the selected foot advances toward the foreground. | [ACE walking lunge reference (PDF)](https://contentcdn.eacefitness.com/cp/pdfs/FitnessMatters/Jan10.pdf) |
| E28 | Halv knäböj | Framifrån | Keeps both feet and both hands on a fixed support; knees travel outward with the squat. | [AAOS Knee Conditioning Program, page 4 (PDF)](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/globalassets/pdfs/2017-rehab_knee.pdf#page=4) |
| E42 | Sidoplanka på bänk med benlyft | Sned vinkel | Shows the upper foot on the bench and the lower forearm supporting on the floor; the selected lower leg moves. | [Related Copenhagen exercise study](https://bmjopensem.bmj.com/content/5/1/e000570) |
| E47 | Enbensknäböj till stol | Framifrån | Keeps the selected standing foot planted, with its knee following the foot toward the chair. | [Norfolk and Waveney Community Health knee pain leaflet (PDF)](https://www.norfolkandwaveneycommunityhealth.nhs.uk/media/xqfl3fv3/knee-pain-leaflet.pdf) |

These sources are the references already recorded for the catalogue entries.
The E42 study covers related Copenhagen variations; it does not validate the
specific repeated lower-leg lift shown here. Geometry tests check contacts and rendering consistency. The drawings have not
undergone anatomical or clinical validation or a beginner comprehension study.
